## Monitoring

The **Monitoring** tab shows the running container live:

- **CPU** against the app's core limit,
- **Memory** as a percentage of its limit,
- **Network in / out**,
- the **process tree**: every process with its PID, user, CPU %, memory (RSS), uptime, and command.

Metrics appear once the app is started.

<Callout type="tip">
Memory near its limit? Raise it in **Settings → Container settings**. For PHP apps this also adds workers.
</Callout>

## Logs

The **Logs** tab streams everything the container prints: your app, Nginx, PHP-FPM, and the scheduler. Use
**Wrap lines**, **Copy visible logs**, and **Clear logs** from the toolbar.

- Known secrets (database passwords etc.) are redacted.
- Docker keeps up to 30 MB of logs per app.
- Your framework's own log files (e.g. `storage/logs/laravel.log`) are in the home — read them from the
  [Terminal](/apps/terminal).
