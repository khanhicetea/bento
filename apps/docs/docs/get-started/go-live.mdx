---
title: Put it online
description: Turn on Bento's edge, point DNS, and publish your app on HTTPS.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point DNS at the server">
    Create an `A` (and `AAAA`, if you have IPv6) record for `shop.example.com` pointing at your server's public IP.
    Certificates can't be issued until DNS resolves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the edge (once per server)">
    Go to **Ingress → Edge**:

    - Tick **Enabled**. Keep **Bind** `0.0.0.0`, **HTTP** `80`, **HTTPS** `443`.
    - Under **Certificates · ACME**, enter your **Contact email**. Leave the directory URL empty for Let's Encrypt.
    - Save.

    Make sure your firewall allows ports 80 and 443.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the app">
    Open the app and press **Publish**. Bento checks the app is running and healthy, then turns its route on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Visit `https://shop.example.com`. The first request may take a few seconds while the certificate is issued.

## Publish vs. start

These are separate on purpose:

- **Start / Stop** controls whether the app runs (including its cron jobs and workers).
- **Publish / Unpublish** controls whether the public can reach it.

Unpublishing takes the site offline but keeps jobs and workers running — handy for maintenance. Stopping always
unpublishes first.

## No public ports?

If your server sits behind NAT, or you'd rather not open ports, use a [Cloudflare Tunnel](/ingress/tunnel) instead of
the edge.
